# 5. Guides, videos and Dina

How people learn to use the app, and the Dina and Toby characters.

## Built
- Branded how-to guides as PDFs for teacher and student, downloadable in the app.
- Six student how-to videos with Dina and Toby and a Spanish voiceover.
- Guide page: videos as small tiles that open to play, written guide in view.
- Dina portrait and logo; Toby without a bow.
- Update brief for the teacher as an easy-to-read PDF.

## Where
`src/screens/Guide.tsx`, `public/guides/`, `src/components/Dina.tsx`, guide and video build scripts in `scripts/`.

## Open
- Guides and videos do not yet cover the newest lesson room features (whiteboard, groups, releasing words and verbs).
