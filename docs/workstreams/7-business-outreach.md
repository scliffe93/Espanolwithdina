# 7. Business and outreach

Taking the platform to other tutors and schools.

## Built
- Personalised pitch page in the app (`#/pitch`).
- Outreach email in HTML and plain text, with a sending guide.
- Per-student pricing with a schools tier.
- A sales deck, a five-year profit model and a who-to-reach workbook.

## Where
`src/screens/` (pitch), `public/pitch/`. The profit model and the who-to-reach workbook are kept outside this repository on purpose, because it is public.

## Rules
- No personal email addresses for sole-trader tutors.
- No paid services without the owner's approval.
