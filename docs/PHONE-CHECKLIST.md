# Ten-minute check on a real phone

Everything in the app is tested automatically in a browser at phone size (`scripts/phone-check.cjs` walks 24 screens
on six phone and tablet sizes). A few things only a real device can prove, because they depend on the phone's own
voice, microphone, notifications and Home Screen. This is the list. Do it once on an iPhone and once on an Android
phone, logged in as a student.

Tick each one. If something fails, open **Account → Troubleshooting**, tap **Copy report**, and send it over.

## Getting in (2 minutes)

- [ ] Open the site in **Safari** (iPhone) or **Chrome** (Android) and log in.
- [ ] iPhone: **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Android: **Install the app** on the Account page.
- [ ] Open the app from the Home Screen icon. You are still logged in.
- [ ] The bottom menu is one row: four tabs and **Más**. Tapping **Más** opens the rest.

## Sound (2 minutes)

- [ ] **Account → Troubleshooting → Test the voice.** You hear "Hola, soy Dina" to the end, not cut off.
- [ ] **Verbos → Start.** Answer one correctly: the chime plays, then the whole answer is read out.
- [ ] Tap a verb in the list, then tap three forms quickly. Each new one replaces the last; none stays silent.
- [ ] With the phone on **silent**: note whether you still hear the voice. (iPhones usually stay silent. That is the phone, not the app.)

## Speaking (2 minutes)

- [ ] In a verb session, find a typed question and tap the **microphone**. The phone asks for permission once.
- [ ] Say the answer. It appears in the box and is checked.
- [ ] **Juegos → Say It** (if it is listed). Tap the big microphone, say the form, see it marked.
- [ ] If the microphone button is missing, or says speaking is not available: note which phone and whether you opened the app from the Home Screen or the browser. Some iPhones only allow it in Safari.

## Notifications (3 minutes)

- [ ] **Account → Notifications → Turn on notifications.** A test notification arrives within a few seconds.
- [ ] Set the **Daily reminder** to five minutes from now and switch it on. Close the app. It arrives within about ten minutes of that time.
- [ ] Tap the notification. It opens the app on the verb gym.
- [ ] As the teacher on another device, set that student a piece of homework. The student's phone is told.

## Feel (1 minute)

- [ ] **Juegos → Metro Rush.** The train slides smoothly; tapping a carriage answers straight away.
- [ ] **Juegos → Pairs.** Match five pairs quickly. Nothing freezes between taps.
- [ ] **Verbos → Grid drill.** Tap into a blank: the page does not zoom in, and the accent buttons type into that blank.
- [ ] Put the app in the background for a minute and come back. You are where you left off.
