A school is any client with more than one teacher, or anyone who wants the school plan's extras.

**Definitions and terms**

- An **active student** is a student account with a lesson booked or homework set in that calendar month. Students on holiday cost nothing.
- Billed monthly in arrears from the app's own count, by direct debit or card. **Annual plans** are paid up front and get two months free.
- **Thirty-day money-back** on the subscription, not on the set-up.
- The set-up covers the branded copy, the character, the accounts, the guides, an onboarding call and loading the first students. For a school it also covers the admin seat and a session for the team.
- Prices exclude VAT where it applies.

**Why per student**

A flat monthly fee charges a school with 150 students the same as a tutor with ten, and leaves most of the value with the school. Per-student pricing keeps the price near one lesson's fee for a tutor with a dozen students, and makes a school's bill a small fraction of one teacher's time. It also means our income grows when a client's does, which is the right incentive for both sides.
